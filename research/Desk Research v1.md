## RQ1

(large retail) In one retailer with 37 stores, about 65% of roughly 370,000 store inventory records were inaccurate, but the study does not break errors down by receiving step.
https://pubsonline.informs.org/doi/fpi/10.1287/mnsc.1070.0789

(large retail) The ECR report names wrong shipments and unrecorded additional deliveries as causes of inaccurate records and recommends, without testing, stricter quality control in the store goods receiving area.
https://orca.cardiff.ac.uk/142664/1/84486%20ECR%20Inventory%20Inaccuracy%20Report.pdf

(vendor documentation) Square for Retail lets users mark a whole purchase order "Receive All" or "Receive None" or enter a partial quantity per row, and the page does not describe recording damaged or wrong items.
https://squareup.com/help/gb/en/article/8260-receive-a-purchase-order-from-your-square-dashboard-and-square-for-retail

(trade press, 13 store grocery chain, 2002, one company quote) A manager said that with paper receiving, shipping errors were rarely detected before reaching the register, and that other companies, which send receiving batches to head office, do not find problems until the next day.
https://www.supermarketnews.com/archive/new-dsd-system-cuts-grocers-shipping-errors

Not found: any study measuring at which receiving step (open order, count, compare, record, confirm) errors or delays occur, how often interruptions happen, or how often "receive all" is used without a real count.

## RQ2

(two case companies: a business to business spare parts distributor and an engineering supplies wholesaler that also sells online to consumers) Reception errors such as wrong delivery, over delivery, missing delivery notes and substituted items formed one of six discrepancy categories.
https://orca.cardiff.ac.uk/120277/1/DS%20IRI_final.pdf

(vendor opinion, supplier side, Walmart) SPS Commerce lists retailer receiving errors, mixed shipments, pack quantity mismatches and overbilled invoices as shortage causes and claims shortages are nearly 80% of revenue lost to Walmart deductions, which is not independently verified.
https://www.spscommerce.com/community/articles/7-most-common-root-causes-for-retailer-shortages

(trade press, 13 store grocery chain, 2002) The manager said that once product is on the shelf there is very little chance of getting proper credit, and checking scans against electronic orders stopped receipt of unordered goods.
https://www.supermarketnews.com/archive/new-dsd-system-cuts-grocers-shipping-errors

(large retail) Unrecorded extra deliveries create stock that physically exists but is missing from the system.
https://orca.cardiff.ac.uk/142664/1/84486%20ECR%20Inventory%20Inaccuracy%20Report.pdf

Not found: how small or independent shops resolve short, over, wrong or damaged deliveries (claims, credit notes, calls), and any rate of damaged items at receipt in small retail.

## RQ3

(healthcare, 60 outpatient clinics) 260 of 6,930 manually reentered glucose results (3.7%) differed from the interfaced value.
https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6351970

(healthcare, one urology service) Manual transcription had a 2.8% error rate, with text fields (4.7%) more error prone than numeric fields (1.1%).
https://bmjopen.bmj.com/content/3/5/e002406

(lab experiment, 195 students, abstract only) Visually checking entered data was not significantly better than single entry, while double entry produced fewer errors.
https://online210.psych.wisc.edu/wp-content/uploads/PSY-210_Unit_Materials/PSY-210_Unit05_Materials/Barchard_abstract_CHB_2011.pdf

(public health immunization, Canada, summary page) Barcode scanning had 0% errors versus 1.7% for drop down menus at one site, and 0% versus 5.6% for typing at another site.
https://www.technet-21.org/en/resources/journal-article/the-integration-of-barcode-scanning-technology-into-canadian-public-health-immunization-settings

Not found: any study of paper to system reentry, handheld entry error rates, or delay between counting and entry in retail receiving or small shops.

## RQ4

(large retail, European retailers; sources disagree on whether 7 or 8 took part) Causes include wrong shipments, additional deliveries, checkout errors, theft, damage and manual adjustments, and when the handheld shows no stock, staff may assume the item is not in the store.
https://orca.cardiff.ac.uk/142664/1/84486%20ECR%20Inventory%20Inaccuracy%20Report.pdf

(simulation of one store) Among misplacement types, misplacement during shelf replenishment had the largest effect on the system showing less than reality, while case packs were the largest single driver, through handling errors such as scanning a single item as a case pack.
https://orca.cardiff.ac.uk/id/eprint/150865/1/Best%20et%20al%20%282022%20-%20IJPDLM%29.pdf

(distribution centre of a midsize apparel retailer) The authors separate receiving and shipping transaction errors from theft, spoilage and internal moves, and argue without measuring that daily accuracy swings erode record confidence so managers triple check and add buffers.
https://core.ac.uk/download/213060804.pdf

(analytical model, generic retail) Execution errors include wrong accounting of inbound shipments from product identification or labeling mistakes, and checkout workarounds like scanning one packet's barcode to register a sale of ten packets.
https://core.ac.uk/download/52922895.pdf

(university news article on large retailer data, secondary source) Store managers reportedly brushed off discrepancies as an annoyance because fixing them was not cost effective.
https://www.kenan-flagler.unc.edu/news/retailers-dirty-little-secret/

(simulation, supply chain workers, abstract only) Inventory policies that usually dampen order and inventory variance amplification did not work when record inaccuracy came from worker behaviour under workload pressure.
https://emerald.com/insight/content/doi/10.1108/IJPDLM-09-2013-0240/full/html

Not found: a dedicated study measuring how much staff or owners trust system stock numbers, how often they override them or check the shelf instead, and any source on small grocery or stationery shops.

## RQ5

(vendor documentation) Square documents partial receiving on Dashboard and POS, but not short, over or damaged reasons, undoing a receipt, or barcode scanning for receiving.
https://squareup.com/help/gb/en/article/8260-receive-a-purchase-order-from-your-square-dashboard-and-square-for-retail

(vendor documentation, transfers not purchase orders) Shopify allows bulk or partial receiving, records rejected items without changing stock, lets users add an optional note to any receiving action, and lets users edit received quantities afterward.
https://help.shopify.com/en/manual/products/inventory/transfers/receive-inventory

(vendor documentation) Shopify POS with Stocky receives purchase orders on the POS device by barcode scan or manual count with a Reject option, but names no reason codes and no way to edit a received order.
https://help.shopify.com/en/manual/sell-in-person/shopify-pos/inventory-management/stocky/pos-inventory-management/receiving-purchase-orders

(vendor documentation) Zoho Inventory supports entering quantities, scanning items, and editing or deleting a saved purchase receive, but does not mention short, over, damaged or rejected items.
https://www.zoho.com/inventory/help/purchase-orders/purchase-receive.html

(vendor documentation) Loyverse uses a "To Receive" field or "Mark All Received" with a partial status, and does not mention discrepancy reasons, undoing, or barcode receiving.
https://help.loyverse.com/help/how-purchase-orders-and-suppliers

(vendor documentation) inFlow lets each line be received individually while the order stays open, and its mobile app supports receiving by tap or scan, with no mention of discrepancy reasons or reversing a receipt.
https://www.inflowinventory.com/support/cloud/how-do-i-partially-receive-products-on-a-purchase-order-in-inflow-cloud

(vendor documentation, NuORDER purchase orders only) Lightspeed Retail handles outstanding quantities by repeating receiving and supports barcode check in, with no mention of discrepancies, editing or mobile receiving.
https://retail-support.lightspeedhq.com/hc/articles/9172569358363

Not found: any product page documenting structured short, over or damaged reason codes, over receiving, or damaged goods handling, and no coverage of standard Lightspeed, Odoo, Cin7, Sortly, Clover or QuickBooks Commerce.

## RQ6

(large retail, 37 stores, abstract only) 65% of inventory records were inaccurate, with audits reducing and store complexity increasing inaccuracy.
https://econpapers.repec.org/RePEc:inm:ormnsc:v:54:y:2008:i:4:p:627-641

(large retail, about 100 stores, 233,318 SKUs) 59.54% of audited SKUs were inaccurate (grocery 63.39%) and correcting records raised test store sales by 3.83% to 8.38%, while receiving was not analysed as a driver.
https://orca.cardiff.ac.uk/142664/1/84486%20ECR%20Inventory%20Inaccuracy%20Report.pdf

(grocery, 11 stores, abstract only) Inaccuracy rose with inventory level, restocking frequency and perishability, and audits produced an 11% store wide sales lift.
https://web3.arxiv.org/abs/2506.05357

(two case companies: a business to business spare parts distributor and an engineering supplies wholesaler that also sells online to consumers) Discrepancy claims fell into reception, accounting, warehouse, damage, shipment and design or quality errors, and prior research cited reports inaccuracy in 20% to 67% of SKUs.
https://orca.cardiff.ac.uk/120277/1/DS%20IRI_final.pdf

(distribution centre of a midsize apparel retailer) Inaccurate receipt documents during receiving create transaction errors, and frequent replenishment creates more chances for them.
https://core.ac.uk/download/213060804.pdf

(PhD thesis, modelling) Undetected stock loss hurt performance most, and small losses accumulating over time can cause severe stockouts.
https://dspace.mit.edu/handle/1721.1/36193

Not found: a verified figure for what share of inventory inaccuracy comes from receiving or supplier delivery discrepancies.

## Limits

- No source covers small or independent grocery or stationery shops; almost all evidence is from large chains, distribution centres, simulations, healthcare or vendors.
- All sources are evidence about other people and organizations, not about Shelfwise users, and vendor pages describe what documentation says, not proven absence of features.
- Several rows rest on abstract only reads (DeHoratius and Raman, Barchard and Pace, Bruccoleri et al., Rekik et al. arXiv), a summary page (Canadian barcode studies) or a news article (Mersereau) whose original papers were not opened.
- Chuang and Oliva (2015) on causes and labor effects of record inaccuracy was blocked and not used.
- The SPS Commerce figures are a vendor claim with a commercial interest, and the Supermarket News source is a single 2002 company quote.
