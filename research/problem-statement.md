# Problem statement (draft for review)

Sources: brief.md, claude/Dashboard v1.md. All user evidence in the dashboard is simulated class practice data, not real evidence. External research is about other people and organizations, not Shelfwise users.

## Problem 1 · Counts get lost between shelf and app
Problem: Stock keepers count deliveries away from the computer and carry the numbers on paper, phone notes, photos or voice, so numbers get lost, mixed up or recounted before they reach the app.
Reason: Counts travel on paper, phone, photos and voice; 4 of 5 were interrupted (Dashboard v1, RQ3, RQ1).

## Problem 2 · Stock is entered late
Problem: Counts are often entered into the app hours or days after the delivery arrives, so the recorded stock is wrong in the meantime.
Reason: 20 minutes to three days pass between counting and entry (Dashboard v1, RQ1).

## Problem 3 · Differences lose their reason and are never settled
Problem: When a delivery is short, extra, damaged or wrong, users record only a number, so the reason is lost and follow up with the supplier happens outside the app and often never finishes.
Reason: P1, P3 and P5 record only a number and never finished follow up (Dashboard v1, RQ2).

## Problem 4 · Mistakes after confirming can't be traced, so users stop trusting the number
Problem: 2 of 5 (P1, P3) say they could not fix a confirmed receipt, so they correct stock by hand without a trace, and then they check the shelf instead of trusting the app.
Reason: Manual fixes leave no trace and trust drops for edited items (Dashboard v1, RQ1, RQ4).
