## 1. Project
Shelfwise is a desktop app that helps small shops (grocery, stationery) track their stock; shop owners and stock keepers use it.
The client asked us to find out why users talk about wrong stock and differences after a delivery arrives.
Our current job is to understand the problem in receiving a delivery and recording it in stock (steps F1–F5), not to design.
