## Persona 1 · Shop owner
- Goal: the stock in the app matches the real stock after a delivery. I don't know if this differs from the stock keeper's goal. (brief.md, "Why now", K5)
- What is hard: when a delivery is short, extra, damaged or wrong, the reason is lost and follow up with the supplier often never finishes. I don't know if the owner or the stock keeper does the follow up. (problem-statement.md, Problem 3; Dashboard v1, RQ2)
- What is hard: users don't fully trust the app's number, especially for items they edited by hand. I don't know if this is different for owners. (Dashboard v1, RQ4)
- What they do today: P1 and P2 check the shelf or storeroom before acting. I don't know their roles. (Dashboard v1, RQ4)
- I don't know: how often owners receive deliveries themselves, or which decisions they make from the stock number.

## Persona 2 · Stock keeper
- Goal: get a delivery from the cartons into the app correctly. This is our reading of the F1 to F5 flow, not a stated goal. (brief.md, F1 to F5)
- What is hard: counting happens in a back room or basement, away from the computer, and numbers on paper, phone notes, photos or voice get lost or mixed. 4 of 5 were interrupted while counting. (problem-statement.md, Problem 1; Dashboard v1, RQ1, RQ3)
- What is hard: counts reach the app 20 minutes to three days later, and P1 and P3 say they could not fix a mistake after confirming. (problem-statement.md, Problems 2 and 4; Dashboard v1, RQ1)
- What they do today: count first, enter later; P4 is the exception and uses a scanner at the desk with no paper. (Dashboard v1, RQ1, RQ3)
- I don't know: whether owners also do these tasks. brief.md says the owner or the stock keeper uses the app (K2).

Sources: interviews: I don't know which; no file gives a role per participant. The full pool is P1 to P5, 5 simulated interviews. Desk research (via Dashboard v1): PMC and BMJ Open (healthcare), Barchard and Pace (lab study), TechNet 21 (public health), Square docs.

Reason: 4 of 5 were interrupted while counting (P1, P2, P3, P5). Their roles are unknown. (Dashboard v1, RQ1)
