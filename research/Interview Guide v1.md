# Interview Guide v1
Version: v1 · Date: 2026-11-02 · Time: 19:30 · Created by: Tannaz

| # | Question | RQ ID | What we are listening for |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Tell me about the last delivery you received. Walk me through what you did from the moment it arrived until the stock was updated. | RQ1 | The real order of steps compared with F1 to F5: steps skipped, merged, reordered or added |
| 2 | Think of a delivery that took longer than usual to record. What happened? | RQ1 | Which step the delay happened in, and what caused it (driver waiting, interruptions, customers) |
| 3 | Tell me about a time you found a mistake in a delivery record after you had finished it. How did you find it? | RQ1 | Which step the mistake came from, and how long it took to notice. Listen for shortcuts that record what was ordered instead of what arrived |
| 4 | Tell me about the last time what arrived did not match what you ordered. What did you do? | RQ2 | Type of difference (short, extra, wrong, damaged), and where it was written down: in the app, on paper, nowhere |
| 5 | After you found that difference, what happened next until it was settled? | RQ2 | Who was involved, how long it took, how it ended, records kept outside the app |
| 6 | Tell me about a difference you discovered later, not at delivery. How did you work out where it came from? | RQ2 | Sources they name (supplier, counting, entry, sales, loss) and how sure they were |
| 7 | Walk me through the last time you counted a delivery. Where were you, and how did you keep track of the numbers? | RQ3 | Paper, memory, phone, labels; distance between shelf and computer |
| 8 | Tell me how the counts got from the shelf into the app that day. | RQ3 | Time gap, who entered them, retyping, things forgotten on the way |
| 9 | Tell me about a time a count got lost, mixed up or had to be redone. What happened? | RQ3 | Errors that come from moving numbers from shelf to desk, and interruptions |
| 10 | Tell me about the last time the app showed a number that did not match the shelf. What did you do? | RQ4 | How they found it, whether they trusted the app or their own count |
| 11 | Before you reorder or tell a customer an item is in stock, what do you do to check? Tell me about the last time. | RQ4 | Trust shown through behaviour: double checking, ignoring the app, relying on memory |
| 12 | Tell me about the last time you changed a stock number in the app by hand. What led to it? | RQ4 | Causes users name, how often they correct, what they do when unsure |
