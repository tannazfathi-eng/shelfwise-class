# Solution directions
Version: v1 · Date: 7 November 2026 · Time: 14:00 · Created by: Claude

Sources: claude/problem-statement.md, claude/Dashboard v1.md, claude/persona.md. All participant evidence is simulated class practice data, not real evidence (Dashboard v1). "partly supported by" means a participant already does something close to the direction. We do not know whether any direction works for real users.

Problem 1: Counts get lost between shelf and app (problem-statement.md, Problem 1)
How might we…? How might we help counts get from where the stock is counted into the app without being lost, mixed up or counted again?
1.1 Capture each count at the place where counting happens, so the number is written only once. [no evidence]
Serves: Stock keeper. Reason: counting happens in a back room or basement, and P5's basement has no signal (Dashboard v1, RQ3).
1.2 Keep track of how far a count has got, so an interruption does not mean starting again. [no evidence]
Serves: Stock keeper. Reason: 4 of 5 were interrupted and P1 and P3 recounted; no file shows anyone tracking progress (Dashboard v1, RQ1).
1.3 Bring the counting to where the app is, so counting and recording happen together. [partly supported by P4]
Serves: Stock keeper. Reason: P4 uses a scanner at the desk with no paper, but most count away from the computer (Dashboard v1, RQ3).
Chosen direction and my reason: 1.3. The only direction with evidence; P4 scans at the desk with no paper (Dashboard v1, RQ3). Against it: P1, P3 and P5 count in a back room or basement, away from the computer (Dashboard v1, RQ3).

Problem 2: Stock is entered late (problem-statement.md, Problem 2)
How might we…? How might we keep the stock number honest in the time between a delivery arriving and its counts being entered?
2.1 Make it clear that a delivery has arrived but is not recorded yet, so nobody treats the number as final. [no evidence]
Serves: Shop owner. Reason: late entry is one of the two causes that appear most; no file shows anyone marking this state (Dashboard v1, RQ4).
2.2 Let a delivery be recorded bit by bit while counting goes on, and keep the part already entered safe. [partly supported by P4]
Serves: Stock keeper. Reason: P4 counts and records at the same time; half finished entries get lost for P2, P4 and P5 (Dashboard v1, RQ1).
2.3 Use the supplier's delivery note as a temporary number until the real count is entered. [no evidence]
Serves: Shop owner. Reason: no file shows anyone doing this, and how often stock is received without a real count is not known (Dashboard v1, RQ1).
Chosen direction and my reason: 2.2. The only direction with evidence; P4 counts and records at the same time, and partial entries were lost for P2, P4 and P5 (Dashboard v1, RQ1).

Problem 3: Differences lose their reason and are never settled (problem-statement.md, Problem 3)
How might we…? How might we keep the reason for a short, extra, damaged or wrong item attached to the delivery until it is settled with the supplier?
3.1 Record what kind of difference it is together with the number. [partly supported by P4]
Serves: Shop owner. Reason: P4 writes what happened in the notes field, while P1, P3 and P5 record only a number (Dashboard v1, RQ2).
3.2 Keep each open difference in view until it is settled. [no evidence]
Serves: Shop owner; I don't know if the stock keeper does the follow up (persona.md). Reason: follow up for P1, P3 and P5 never finished (Dashboard v1, RQ2).
3.3 Pass each difference to the person who settles it. [partly supported by P2, P4]
Serves: Shop owner; I don't know if the stock keeper does the follow up (persona.md). Reason: P2 settles through the driver and P4 passes notes to the accountant, outcome unknown (Dashboard v1, RQ2).
Chosen direction and my reason: 3.1. P4 writes what happened in the notes, while P1, P3 and P5 record only a number (Dashboard v1, RQ2). Not 3.3, because the outcome of P4's notes to the accountant is unknown (Dashboard v1, RQ2) and we don't know who does the follow up (persona.md).

Problem 4: Mistakes after confirming can't be traced, so users stop trusting the number (problem-statement.md, Problem 4)
How might we…? How might we let users fix a confirmed delivery in a way that leaves a trace, so they can trust the number again?
4.1 Allow a confirmed delivery to be corrected, with the old and new number both kept. [no evidence]
Serves: Stock keeper. Reason: P1 and P3 say they could not fix a confirmed receipt; I don't know if the app really has no undo (Dashboard v1, RQ1).
4.2 Help users catch mistakes before they confirm, while they can still change them. [no evidence]
Serves: Stock keeper. Reason: 3 of 5 found a mistake only after confirming (Dashboard v1, RQ1).
4.3 Show where each stock number came from, such as a delivery or a hand edit. [no evidence]
Serves: Shop owner. Reason: trust drops for items users edited by hand, P3 and P5 (Dashboard v1, RQ4).
Chosen direction and my reason: None chosen. All three directions have no evidence, so this goes to the open questions list.

Counts: 4 partly supported (1.3, 2.2, 3.1, 3.3), 8 no evidence.
Note: all three choices rest mostly on one simulated participant, P4.
